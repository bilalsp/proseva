# ProSeva (Pro-Say-va)

ProSeva is a platform designed to connect service seekers with trusted professionals. Inspired by platforms like Justdial and Leboncoin, ProSeva aims to simplify the discovery and booking of local services from electricians and plumbers to tutors and consultants through a robust, scalable microservices architecture. Its mission is to empower professionals and service-based businesses by providing them with a digital presence and reliable customer reach.


> [!WARNING]
>
> This project is in a very early stage of development. 
> Features are incomplete and the codebase is actively evolving. 
>
